## 🔑 CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### #1: Execute the Free Tier First
Without a free tier, you're asking cold prospects to pay. Conversion will be 2-3% (vs 10-15%).
**Risk:** Over-engineering free tier. Keep it simple. MVP is fine.

### #2: Recruit 20+ Partners by Day 21
Partners are your distribution. Each partner brings 5-20 clients.
**Risk:** Waiting for partners to self-discover. You have to recruit proactively.

### #3: Monitor Weekly Metrics
Don't wait 30 days to see if strategy is working. Track daily:
- Signup conversion rate
- Free → paid conversion
- CAC by channel
- Partner activation

**Risk:** Ignoring red flags. If conversion <5%, diagnose immediately.

### #4: Don't Let Features Block Launches
Expense tracking is nice-to-have. Free tier is table-stakes.
**Risk:** Delaying free tier launch to add features.

### #5: Content is Compound
Blog posts, videos, guides take 3-4 weeks to show ROI.
**Risk:** Stopping marketing if no immediate results. Be patient, compound wins.

## 🚨 RED FLAGS (If You See These, Pause & Diagnose)

### Red Flag #1: Free Signups <50/day by Day 7
- **Diagnosis:** Landing page not converting or ads not running
- **Fix:** Check landing page bounce rate. Improve CTA. Increase ad spend.

### Red Flag #2: Free → Paid Conversion <5% by Day 14
- **Diagnosis:** Free tier is too good (people stay free forever) or upsell messaging bad
- **Fix:** Reduce free tier limits. Improve upgrade prompts. Survey churned users.

### Red Flag #3: 0 Accountant Partners by Day 14
- **Diagnosis:** Recruitment strategy not working or compensation too low
- **Fix:** Increase commission (₱50 → ₱75). Shift to paid influencers. Try direct calls.

### Red Flag #4: Engineering Behind Schedule by Week 3
- **Diagnosis:** Scope creep or technical complexity underestimated
- **Fix:** Cut features. Hire contractors. Extend timeline.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Should we launch all 3 pillars at once?**  
A: No. Free tier first (week 1-2), then partners (week 2-3), then features (week 5+). Sequence matters.

**Q: What if we don't have budget for ads?**  
A: Organic + partner referrals are free. Ads accelerate but aren't required. Start without ads, add if CAC allows.

**Q: Can we skip the partnership program and just do ads?**  
A: Yes, but you'll grow slower. Partners are highest-ROI channel (₱30-50 CAC vs ₱200+ for cold ads).

**Q: What if engineering says 12 weeks isn't enough?**  
A: Launch MVP. Free tier + expense tracking (8 weeks). Bank reconciliation (later). Don't let perfection block good.

**Q: How do we measure success?**  
A: 2,000 subscriptions + ₱300K MRR by November 30. Check monthly targets in the playbooks.

**Q: What if we miss targets?**  
A: Diagnose by day 14. Free tier conversion low? Fix landing page. Partners not recruiting? Raise commission. Iterate weekly.
