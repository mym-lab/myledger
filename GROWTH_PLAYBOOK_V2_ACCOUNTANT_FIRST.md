### Annual Billing Strategy

**The Mechanism:**
- 20% discount when paid annually (not monthly)
- **Example:** Starter ₱299/mo × 12 months = ₱3,588 annually (vs ₱4,308 monthly)
- One-click toggle at upgrade: "Pay monthly" vs "Pay annually (save ₱720)"

**Why This Works:**

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Retention** | Annual commitment → 80% lower churn |
| **Revenue clarity** | Easier to forecast ARR |
| **Competitive** | Customers see "save 2 months" (anchoring) |
| **Cash flow** | Upfront annual payment → better working capital |
| **Discount is real** | 20% = ₱3,588 vs ₱4,308 (user sees actual savings) |

**Conversion Strategy:**

At moment of upgrade:
```
User chooses Starter (₱299/mo)
↓
CTA: "Lock in your price. Pay annually, save ₱720."
[Pay Monthly ₱299/mo]  [Pay Annually ₱2,994]
↓
If annual selected:
├─ Message: "Great! You save 2 months of fees."
├─ Note: "Annual plan renews on [DATE]. Cancel anytime."
└─ Email: Renewal reminder 30 days before
```

**Target: 30-40% of users choose annual** (vs 10-15% typical SaaS)

### Red Flags (If You See These, Diagnose)

**Red Flag #1: Accountants signing up but not adding clients**
- **Diagnosis:** Onboarding friction or perceived complexity
- **Fix:** Simplify COA import, add video walkthrough, do live onboarding calls

**Red Flag #2: Free → Paid conversion <3% by day 14**
- **Diagnosis:** Free tier too generous OR upsell messaging bad
- **Fix:** Check free tier usage. If high, reduce free limits. Test new CTA messaging.

**Red Flag #3: Ads have CAC >₱150**
- **Diagnosis:** Wrong audience, bad landing page, or wrong messaging
- **Fix:** Test different landing pages (accountant vs SME vs freelancer). Test different copy. Lower daily budget and optimize.

**Red Flag #4: Accountants converting to white-label <10%**
- **Diagnosis:** White-label ROI unclear or too hard to set up
- **Fix:** Create ROI calculator (show exact revenue impact). Simplify white-label setup. Offer to do it for them.

**Red Flag #5: Churn >10%/month**
- **Diagnosis:** Feature gap, poor support, or pricing misalignment
- **Fix:** Survey churned customers. Build the top feature requested. Check support response times.
