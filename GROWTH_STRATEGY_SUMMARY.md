## THE OPPORTUNITY

Your current pricing and feature set are good, but **you're leaving significant money on the table**:

1. **No free tier** → High friction at signup (competitors have free options)
2. **No accountant incentives** → Accountants are natural distributors (30-40% of SME SMEs use accountants)
3. **Missing critical features** → Expense tracking + bank reconciliation are table-stakes (unlock ₱500K+ in incremental ARR)
4. **No distribution channels** → You're relying on organic growth (slow, expensive)

**Fix these 4 things, and you can hit 2,000 subscriptions in 90 days.**

---

## THE STRATEGY (3 PILLARS)

### PILLAR 1: Free Tier (Removes Friction)
**Messaging:** "Try free. Upgrade when you're ready."

- ✅ Free plan: 5 invoices/month, basic reports
- ✅ Removes risk for new users (most SMBs are risk-averse)
- ✅ Expected impact: 2-3x signup volume
- ✅ Free → paid conversion: 10-15% within 30 days
- ✅ Timeline: 1.5 weeks

**Examples:**
- Freelancer: Signs up free, creates 5 invoices, needs 6th → sees "Upgrade to ₱199" prompt → converts

### PILLAR 2: Accountant Referral Program (Distribution)
**Messaging:** "Refer your clients, earn ₱50/client/month forever."

- ✅ Partners earn passive income (₱250-1K/month with 5-20 referrals)
- ✅ Zero acquisition cost (pay only for referrals that stick)
- ✅ Viral loop: 1 accountant = 10-20 clients
- ✅ Expected impact: 100+ partners × 10 clients = 1,000+ referred clients
- ✅ Timeline: 2 weeks to recruit & onboard

**Examples:**
- Accountant A signs up, refers 5 clients in week 1, starts earning ₱250/month
- By month 3: 30 referred clients = ₱1,500/month in passive commissions
- Accountant B hears about it, signs up, repeats

### PILLAR 3: Feature Enhancements (Unlock Willingness-to-Pay)
**Messaging:** "Better accounting software, lower cost."

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Expense Tracking**
- Let freelancers/SME owners upload receipts, auto-extract via OCR
- Unlock ₱99 → ₱499 upsell (₱400/year per customer)
- Expected impact: ₱500K+ incremental ARR

**Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Bank Reconciliation**
- Connect to BPI, BDO, Metabank via Plaid
- Auto-match bank transactions to expenses/invoices
- Save accountants 5-10 hours/month
- Unlock ₱499 → ₱2,500 (accountant plan) upsell
- Expected impact: ₱200K+ incremental ARR

**Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): BIR Form Pre-Filling**
- Auto-populate 1200, 2550M, other BIR forms
- Differentiate vs competitors (none do this)
- Expected impact: ₱150K+ incremental ARR

## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Free → Paid Conversion Too Low
**Mitigation:**
- Set threshold: If conversion <8% by day 14, pause new signups
- Increase upsell triggers (improve messaging)
- Run cohort analysis: Why aren't users converting?
- A/B test pricing ($99 vs $149 vs $199)

### Risk 2: Partner Program Doesn't Grow
**Mitigation:**
- Track: Partner signup rate + partner activity rate
- If <15 partners by week 3, shift to direct outreach (paid influencers)
- If partners don't refer, increase commission (₱50 → ₱75)
- Create success stories: Showcase top partners (social proof)

### Risk 3: Features Take Longer Than Expected
**Mitigation:**
- Use phased rollout (beta → 50% users → 100%)
- Expense tracking is lower priority than free tier (don't block on it)
- Use contractors if engineering capacity stretched
- MVP approach: Launch with 80% of planned features

### Risk 4: Engineering Doesn't Have Capacity
**Mitigation:**
- Hire contractors for non-core work
- Prioritize ruthlessly: Free tier > Partner program > Features
- Use libraries/no-code where possible (OCR via API, not custom)
